# Zero Miles support

**Email:** support@zeromiles.app

That mailbox is an **operator gate**. Code and this repo do not provision the inbox, SLA, or auto-replies. Someone on the operator side must receive mail before you treat support as live.

## How to delete your account

1. Open Zero Miles and go to **Settings** (gear on Home).
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type `DELETE` (exactly) and confirm.

This calls the server teardown path (`delete_my_account`). It permanently deletes your account and the couple’s shared data. Signing out is not deletion.

If you cannot open the app, email **support@zeromiles.app** from the address on the account and ask for deletion.

## How to sign out

Settings → **Sign out**. Your couple pairing stays in place. Sign in again with the same email to return.

## How to unpair

There is no separate Unpair button. The pairing is the couple.

- **Leave the device / session:** Settings → Sign out (pairing remains).
- **End the pairing and wipe shared data:** Settings → **Delete account** → type `DELETE`. Deleting either partner’s account removes the couple.
- **Need the couple dissolved without using the in-app delete flow:** email **support@zeromiles.app**.

Partner pairing (create or join a 6-character code) is Settings → **Partner**.

## What to include in an email

- The email address on the Zero Miles account
- Android or iOS, and approximate app version
- What you already tried (sign out, delete, pairing code)

Do not send pairing codes, passwords, or photo files unless the operator asks.
